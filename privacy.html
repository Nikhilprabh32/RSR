Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version. We store your profile, a record of every email you send through the tool, and notes you write. That is what makes the outreach tracker work.

We ask Google for permission to send mail as you, and nothing else. We have never requested and cannot obtain permission to read your inbox. We do not sell anything, we run no advertising, and we do not share your data with anyone.

Who we are

Ready, Set, Recruit is a free tool for university students cold-emailing professionals during recruiting. It is operated by an individual, not a company. Contact: support@readysetrecruit.com.

What the app does

You sign in with Google. You paste a LinkedIn profile URL and name a firm. The app reads the person's likely name from the URL text itself, combines it with that firm's known email address format, and shows you an address with a confidence score and the reasoning behind it. It drafts an email from details you gave us. You edit it and press send, and it goes out through your own Gmail account.

It then records that you sent it, so you have a running list of who you have contacted, what you said, and where each conversation stands.

Two things we want to be plain about.

The predicted email address is a guess our software produces from a person's name and their employer's usual format. It is not obtained from LinkedIn and not bought from a data broker. We do not operate a database of professionals and we do not sell contact information.

We store the text of the emails you send. That is what lets you see your own history, and it also means a breach of our database would expose those messages. We think the tradeoff is worth it for a tracker, but you should know it before you use one.

Your résumé

If you upload one, the file is read inside your browser and is never uploaded to us. We store only the short lines you explicitly tick to include in your emails. The file itself never leaves your device, and closing the tab discards it.

What we do not store

  • Anything from your inbox. We have no permission to read it.
  • Your Google password, which we never see.
  • Your Google access token, anywhere on our servers. It is held only in your own browser, in storage scoped to that one tab, it expires in under an hour, and closing the tab discards it. It is never written to our database and never leaves your device except to talk to Google.
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies of any kind.

Google account data

Signing in gives the app a short-lived access token in your browser, used for exactly two things:

  • Your email address and name, so the app knows who you are.
  • gmail.send, so the message you wrote can be sent from your account.

gmail.send is send-only by design. It does not grant the ability to read, search, delete or modify anything in your mailbox, and we deliberately did not request any permission that would. This is also why replies are not detected automatically: knowing that someone wrote back would require reading your inbox, and we chose not to ask for that.

Ready, Set, Recruit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell or transfer it, and we do not allow humans to read it except where you have asked us to help with a support issue, where required by law, or as needed to investigate abuse or a security incident.

Where it lives

The site is hosted by Vercel. The database and sign-in are handled by Supabase, on servers in the United States. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Sending goes through Google. Those four are our only third parties. There are no advertising networks and no analytics services.

Access is restricted at the database level so that each account can read only its own rows. Your contacts are not visible to other students.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a contact removes it and its message history. Deleting your account removes everything associated with you, permanently, within 30 days. We do not keep backups beyond that window for deleted accounts.

Your control

  • Delete a contact or a note from inside the app at any time.
  • Export everything we hold about you. Email support and we will send it.
  • Delete your account from your settings, or by emailing support.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, effective immediately. The app can no longer send as you, though your stored data remains until you delete it.

If you are in a jurisdiction with statutory data rights, such as the EEA, the UK or California, those rights apply and the mechanisms above are how you exercise them. Write to support if anything is unclear.

If you received an email sent through this tool

A student used Ready, Set, Recruit to draft and send you a message from their own account. We may hold your name, work email address and employer in that student's private contact list. You can ask us to delete it, and we will, across every account that holds it. Write to support@readysetrecruit.com and we will confirm when it is done.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting providers, and database access rules restrict each account to its own records. No system is perfectly secure. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

Built for university students, not directed at anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we do with your information, the date at the top will change and the updated policy will appear here before the change takes effect.
